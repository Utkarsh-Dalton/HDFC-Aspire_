**README: HDFC Aspire Case Submission – Gen Z Insurance Strategy via BGMI**
**Project Overview**
This project was developed as part of the HDFC Aspire Case Competition, where the challenge was to design an innovative strategy to position and sell HDFC Life Insurance to Gen Z consumers. Given that Gen Z accounts for 27% of India’s population but has low awareness and adoption of life insurance, our goal was to create a gamified engagement strategy that resonates with their digital-first preferences.

**Key Problem Statement**
Gen Z is underinsured and lacks awareness of life insurance benefits.
Traditional marketing approaches fail to engage this tech-savvy, experience-driven generation.
India has a $17 trillion financial protection gap, highlighting an urgent need for innovative insurance outreach.
Proposed Solution: HDFC LifeCare x BGMI Collaboration
We proposed an in-game insurance feature, HDFC LifeCare, within BGMI (Battlegrounds Mobile India) to educate and engage Gen Z on life insurance. The feature integrates life insurance concepts into BGMI gameplay, making the purchase process interactive and rewarding.

**Key Features of HDFC LifeCare in BGMI**
Gamified insurance mechanics: Players can purchase HDFC LifeCare using in-game currency.
Real-life insurance benefits: Upon a player’s in-game demise, their teammates receive supplies, mimicking real-life financial security.
Multiple policy tiers: Players can choose basic or premium coverage, simulating real-world insurance options.
Market Research & Validation
107 participants surveyed to understand Gen Z’s gaming habits and insurance perceptions.
73% of respondents showed interest in purchasing HDFC LifeCare within BGMI.
59% stated they would consider life insurance if it were affordable and gamified.
**Implementation Plan**
Partnership Setup: Negotiate licensing and branding agreements with BGMI.
In-Game Feature Development: Design, integrate, and beta-test HDFC LifeCare within the game.
Marketing Rollout: Use social media, influencer partnerships, and esports sponsorships to drive engagement.
Performance Tracking: Measure impact through in-game purchases, social media reach, and insurance awareness surveys.
**Expected Impact**
1M+ HDFC LifeCare purchases within six months.
30% increase in website traffic from in-game referral links.
25% boost in social media engagement through gamification campaigns.
**Future Scalability**
Expand to other popular games like Free Fire & Call of Duty Mobile.
Integrate AR/VR-based financial literacy experiences.
Develop a loyalty program with in-game rewards for sustained engagement.
Conclusion**
**This gamification-based approach leverages Gen Z’s digital habits to transform insurance marketing, bridging the financial protection gap while ensuring HDFC Life remains a leading innovator in the insurance sector.
